## How the client screens exist in this canvas

There is no client route in the canvas. The whole canvas is two routes, `/coach` and `/ethereal` (`frontend/src/app/coach/page.tsx:1-15`, `frontend/src/app/ethereal/page.tsx:1-18`), plus `/` which re-exports the studio (`frontend/src/app/page.tsx:1`). The client screens are the `ClientPreview` tab set inside the coach shell (`frontend/src/components/coach/client-preview.tsx:857-978`): Dashboard, Tracker, Timeline/Roadmap, Nutrition, Training Program, Check-In, Habits (`client-preview.tsx:877-887`), plus the standalone tracker that PR-W1 turns into `/me` (`frontend/src/components/coach/client-tracker-preview/tracking-view.tsx:40`, PLAN `icm/03-plan/output/PLAN.md:420`).

Consequence for every table below: the canvas "screen" is a coach-facing preview that renders client markup. The client-facing route, role gate and own-scope data binding do not exist in the canvas today; they are PR-W1.

All 35 briefing shots are coach or studio screens (`icm/04-briefing/shots/index.json`); the closest client screens are `s09-tracker-today/week/trends.png` and `s11-client-preview.png`.
