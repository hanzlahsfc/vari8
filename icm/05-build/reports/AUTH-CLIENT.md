## Guard

`tests/e2e/me-auth-look.spec.ts`, 5 tests, all green. It creates an unarchived `ClientProfile` for `E2E.client` and one CLIENT plus one COACH invitation on the reserved E2E workspace, removes/restores them afterwards, and:

1. in dark and in light (seeded through the client screen's own storage key `coaching-ethereal-appearance-v1`), signs in as the client at `/me/login`, asserts the landing on `/me`, reads the `/me` root background, ink and font, then asserts `/me/login`, `/me/reset-request`, `/me/reset?token=...` and a client `/invite` all mount `.eg-root` with the same `data-mode`, the same computed background, ink and font, a loaded `Ethereal Geist`, `.auth-screen.eg-auth`, and the eg card surface, radius and inset edge;
2. asserts a staff invitation renders `.coach-root` and no `.eg-root`;
3. asserts a signed-out `/me` lands on `/me/login`;
4. asserts a coach signing in at `/me/login` reaches `/coach`;
5. asserts the client reset mail contains `/me/reset?token=` (needs `MAIL_DELIVERY_ADAPTER=dev-file`).

The look assertions fail if the eg root is removed: the spec queries `.eg-root` directly and compares its computed values to the literals above.

## Not done, stated plainly

- **No client invitation email exists in this tree.** `sendMail` is called only by the password reset flow; invitations are created by `lib/workspace/invitations.ts` and no route or mail template builds an accept URL. The "client invitation email" link builder in the card therefore had nothing to change. The acceptance redirect and the sign-in links on `/invite` are routed by role.
- **The post-reset redirect still goes to `/login?reset=1`.** `completeReset` is the shared #130 action and the ruling forbids new server actions; a client who sets a password from `/me/reset` lands on the staff sign-in, signs in, and `/` routes them to `/me`. Changing it would mean threading a return path through the shared form and action.
- **The client screen has no sign-out control.** The client sign-out surface is the `/logout` POST and `signOutAction`; both now land a client on `/me/login`. No UI was invented.
- The reset routes render only when a mail adapter is configured (`MAIL_DELIVERY_ADAPTER=dev-file`); without one they 404 exactly as the staff routes do.
